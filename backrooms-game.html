What is the Backrooms?

The Backrooms is a surreal maze of endless rooms, halls, and unfamiliar spaces. This game turns that idea into a playable browser experience with exploration, survival mechanics, multiplayer features, and different Backrooms-inspired levels.

Level 0 — The Yellow Rooms
Start in the classic yellow Backrooms environment and search for a way forward.
Poolrooms
Explore a quiet tiled environment filled with water, echoes, and a strange sense that something is wrong.
Multiplayer
Play with other people, see other players in the world, and explore together.
PC + Mobile
The game is designed to support both desktop controls and mobile touch controls.

Backrooms Levels

The game includes hostile, neutral, and peaceful environments. Future updates can expand the world with more levels, puzzles, entities, secrets, and unique escape routes.

How to Play

Choose a level, start exploring, look for useful items and objectives, and keep moving. On desktop, keyboard and mouse controls are supported. On mobile, touch movement and actions are supported.
